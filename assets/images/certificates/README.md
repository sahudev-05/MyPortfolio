## How to add or replace your certificates:
1. Save your certificate scan, export, or screenshot (e.g. `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.svg`) into this folder (`assets/images/certificates/`).
2. You can either name your file with the same filename (e.g. replace `aws-cloud-foundations.svg` with your actual file), OR update the `data-img` and `src` path in `index.html` under the `#certifications` section.
3. Any added certificates will automatically display in the interactive carousel and will open in full resolution when tapped in the certificate names list!
